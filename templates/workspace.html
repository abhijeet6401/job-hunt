Deployment checklist

Set secrets in Render’s Environment settings. Never commit them or put them in browser code.

  • AI: XAI_API_KEY, LLM_PROVIDER=xai, and an accessible XAI_MODEL. Groq remains optional.
  • Search: TAVILY_API_KEY. Unique results vary; SEARCH_RESULTS_PER_QUERY controls retrieval breadth.
  • Sheets: GOOGLE_SHEETS_ID and GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON. Share the sheet with the service account.
  • Storage: attach a persistent disk and set JOBHUNT_DATA_DIR to its mount path. Ephemeral instances lose local files on redeploy.
  • Agent: install Playwright Chromium and set ENABLE_APPLICATION_AGENT=true.
