💡 Senior Outreach & Interview Question Playbook

When speaking to a placed senior, you want to ask actionable, specific questions that reveal shortlisting criteria, OA difficulty, technical round focus areas, and culture fit.

📞 Top 8 Essential Questions to Ask Placed Seniors on a Call ▼
  • 1. Online Assessment (OA) Pattern: What was the platform (HackerEarth, Mettl, CodeSignal)? How many questions, what topics (Trees, DP, Graphs, SQL), and what was the cutoff score to get an interview call?
  • 2. Resume Shortlisting Criteria: Did the company filter purely by CGPA, branch, or prior internships? What was the minimum CGPA that got shortlisted?
  • 3. Round-by-Round Breakdown: How many technical rounds were conducted on placement day? Were they 30 mins or 1 hour? Did they ask live coding, system design, or CV projects?
  • 4. Resume Project Grilling: How deeply did the interviewers probe into ML/software projects? Did they check math foundations, edge cases, or deployment?
  • 5. HR & Cultural Fitment: What questions did the HR/Director ask in the final round? Any specific questions regarding relocation, bond, or why this company?
  • 6. PPO vs CDC Drive Dynamics: If they got PPO, how was their intern performance evaluated? If CDC drive, what helped them stand out on Day 1?
  • 7. CTC Breakdown & Work Life: What is the real in-hand/base salary vs joining bonus/RSUs? What is the team culture and typical working hours?
  • 8. Recommended Preparation Resources: What specific LeetCode lists, sheet (Striver SDE / NeetCode), or guesstimate mock resources do they recommend?
💻 Software Development (SDE) Preparation Roadmap ▼
  • Data Structures & Algorithms (DSA): Master Striver's SDE Sheet. Focus on Arrays, Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Trees, Graphs (BFS/DFS, Dijkstra), Dynamic Programming.
  • Core Computer Science: Operating Systems (Concurrency, Deadlocks, Paging), DBMS (SQL queries, Normalization, Indexing), Computer Networks (TCP/IP, HTTP, DNS).
  • Low-Level & High-Level Design: OOP concepts (SOLID principles, Design Patterns), basic microservices, caching (Redis), databases (SQL vs NoSQL).
  • High CTC Target Companies: Google, Microsoft, Databricks, Amazon, Glean, Rippling, Nutanix, Rubrik, Cohesity, Salesforce.
⚡ Quantitative Research & HFT Trading Roadmap ▼
  • Mathematics & Probability: Conditional probability, Bayes' theorem, Markov chains, Martingales, Expected values, Combinatorics. Books: "Heard on the Street", "A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews" (Xinfeng Zhou).
  • Puzzles & Brain Teasers: 50 Challenging Problems in Probability (Frederick Mosteller), Ted-Ed riddles, Brainstellar puzzles.
  • Low Latency C++: C++ memory model, pointers, move semantics, cache locality, multi-threading, network sockets.
  • Top Recruiter Companies: Optiver (₹2.44 Cr), Quadeye (₹93L), Graviton (₹90L), NK Securities (₹1.68 Cr), AlphaGrep (₹73L), Trexquant (₹55L).
📊 Analytics, Data Science & Management Consulting Roadmap ▼
  • Consulting Cases & Guesstimates: Profitability frameworks, Market Entry, Pricing, M&A. Practice with Case in Point and Victor Cheng frameworks.
  • SQL & Data Analysis: Advanced window functions (`ROW_NUMBER`, `DENSE_RANK`, `LEAD/LAG`), self-joins, aggregations, cohort analysis.
  • Machine Learning: Linear/Logistic regression, Decision Trees, XGBoost, Random Forest, Evaluation metrics (Precision, Recall, F1, ROC-AUC), Transformers & GenAI basics.
  • Top Companies: BCG, Bain, McKinsey, L.E.K. Consulting, American Express, Capital One, EXL Service, Axtria, Mastercard, Navi, BlackRock.
